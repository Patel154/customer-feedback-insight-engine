READ THE SCORES CORRECTLY

Evidence, with context.

The external test contains positive and negative Amazon, Yelp and IMDb reviews. It does not measure performance on real neutral reviews.

The internal test includes generated and curated teaching examples. A high internal score does not guarantee the same accuracy on your uploaded data.

Low-confidence predictions are flagged for review. Sarcasm, unfamiliar language and new product domains can still cause mistakes.

THE AI PIPELINE

From raw feedback to useful evidence.

OFFLINE INFERENCE
01

Read & validate

Preview CSV or Excel, select the review column, and separate missing or invalid input from real feedback.

02

Preserve meaning

Expand contractions, retain negations and context words, and apply a consistent offline Snowball stemmer.

03

Learn & classify

Word and character TF-IDF capture phrases and spelling patterns. Compare four classifiers using training-only cross-validation.

04

Explain & prioritize

Analyze aspect-specific clauses, discover LDA topics, and summarize patterns into suggested business actions.

WHY THESE FEATURES?

Words carry context.

“The product is not good.”Negative

Word unigrams, bigrams and trigrams preserve short phrases such as “not good”. Character 3–5 grams add evidence when spelling varies. The vocabulary is learned only from each training fold.

TF-IDFtf(t, d) × [log((1 + N) / (1 + df(t))) + 1]Common terms receive less weight; informative terms receive more.
Macro F1mean(F1_negative, F1_neutral, F1_positive)Each class contributes equally to the model selection score.
